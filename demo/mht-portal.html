Frequently Asked Questions

Have a question? Ask our MHT Assistant bot in the bottom-right corner for instant answers.

How do I apply for eServices on the MHT portal?
Create an account on the ePortal using your Omani civil ID or Resident ID. After logging in, select the desired service, fill in the application form, upload required documents (PDF or JPG), and pay any applicable fees online. You will receive SMS and email notifications on your application status.
What documents are typically required for service applications?
Required documents vary by service but commonly include: a valid Omani national ID or residency card, a recent passport-size photo, relevant certificates or qualifications, proof of payment of fees, and any sector-specific documents such as a building completion certificate (for hotel licensing) or a safety plan (for adventure tourism). Check the specific service page for the exact checklist.
How long does it take to get an application approved?
Processing times vary by service type. Standard licensing applications (tourist guide, hotel) are typically processed within 5–10 business days. Tourism Land Investment applications may take 30–60 business days due to required inspections. You can track your application status in real time through your ePortal account dashboard.
Does the portal support Arabic and English?
Yes. The MHT ePortal fully supports both Arabic and English. Use the language toggle in the navigation bar to switch. All official certificates and documents are issued in Arabic; English translations may be requested for an additional fee.
How do I contact MHT technical support for portal issues?
For technical support, email info@mht.gov.om or call +968 2468 4000. The ePortal technical support hotline is available from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM, Sunday to Thursday. Ministry working hours are 7:30 AM to 2:30 PM, Sunday to Thursday.
